REGULATORY DEFINITION
14 CFR §260.2 defines a "significantly delayed or changed flight" for purposes of the refund rules in Part 260. Relevant triggers include a scheduled arrival at least 3 hours after the original scheduled arrival for a domestic itinerary, or at least 6 hours after the original scheduled arrival for an international itinerary.

EARLY DEPARTURE
The same definition includes a flight scheduled to depart at least 3 hours earlier than its original scheduled departure for a domestic itinerary, or at least 6 hours earlier for an international itinerary. An early departure is different from a departure that is merely late.

OTHER SIGNIFICANT CHANGES
The definition also includes specified changes such as a different origin or destination airport, more connection points, or an involuntary downgrade, subject to the detailed rule text and applicable conditions.

HOW TO USE THIS SOURCE
For the definition of a significantly delayed or changed flight, cite 14 CFR §260.2. For the carrier's refund obligation after cancellation or significant delay/change, see the separate 14 CFR §260.6 source. Do not cite 14 CFR §250.13 as the refund authority.
